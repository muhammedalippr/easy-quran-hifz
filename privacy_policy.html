Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Easy Quran Hifz mobile application (hereinafter referred to as "the App"), package name com.easyquranhifz.app, developed by our team.

1. Information We Collect

We respect your privacy. Easy Quran Hifz does not collect, transmit, or store any personal identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or precise geographic location.

2. Local Device Storage

All app settings and user preferences are stored locally on your device only, including:

  • Selected repeat counts (e.g. 3x, 5x, continuous loop)
  • Selected reciter preference
  • Recently opened Surahs list
  • Welcome instructions visibility preference

None of this preference data is ever uploaded to our servers or shared with any third party.

3. Third-Party Services & Audio Streaming

The App integrates with the following trusted third-party services to deliver core functionality:

  • EveryAyah.com: Public Quranic audio recitations are streamed directly to your device. No personal user data is sent during audio requests.
  • Google AdMob: The app may use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect non-personal device information, advertising IDs, crash logs, and diagnostic data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy to serve relevant ads. You can review Google's policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Permissions Used

The App only requests the minimum permissions strictly necessary for operation:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required solely to stream recitation audio files from EveryAyah and load advertisements.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for users of all ages, including children learning Quran memorization. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. The App complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policies.

6. Security

All external network communications (audio streaming and ad requests) use industry-standard HTTPS encryption in transit.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
